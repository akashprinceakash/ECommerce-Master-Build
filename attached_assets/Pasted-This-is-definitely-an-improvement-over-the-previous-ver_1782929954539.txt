This is **definitely an improvement** over the previous version because you've introduced a body silhouette and the garments are aligned to body zones. However, **it's still not correct from a fashion/UI perspective**.

### What's good ✅

* The shirt is positioned on the torso.
* The trousers are positioned on the lower body.
* The silhouette helps users understand where garments belong.
* The "Body Zones" concept is a nice addition.

### What still looks wrong ❌

#### 1. The shirt is too large

The polo extends well below the waist and covers part of the trousers.

A golf polo should normally end around the waistband or just slightly below it.

#### 2. The trousers start too low

There is a noticeable gap between the shirt and the trouser waistband.

The trouser waistband should sit just under the shirt, creating the appearance of a complete outfit.

#### 3. The proportions don't match

The shirt and trousers appear to be from different scales.

Each garment type should have predefined dimensions relative to the silhouette:

* Shirts should occupy the upper body.
* Trousers should occupy the lower body.
* The width and height should remain consistent across products.

#### 4. The silhouette is too faint

The outline is barely visible, making it difficult to judge whether the outfit fits correctly.

A slightly darker or more visible silhouette would improve usability.

---

## How it should look

The garment placement should resemble this:

```
        Head
         ○

      ┌─────────┐
      │  Polo   │
      │         │
      └─────────┘
          │
     Waistline
          │
      ┌─────────┐
      │Trouser  │
      │         │
      │         │
      └─────────┘
```

There should be little to no visible gap between the polo and the trousers.

---

## Best implementation (recommended)

Instead of manually positioning every product, define **anchor zones** on the silhouette:

* **Head Zone** → Caps
* **Upper Body Zone** → Polos, T-Shirts, Jackets
* **Lower Body Zone** → Trousers, Shorts, Skorts
* **Feet Zone** → Shoes (future)

Each product should automatically:

* Snap to its designated body zone.
* Scale to a predefined size.
* Align to the center of that zone.

This ensures every outfit looks balanced regardless of the product image.

---

### Overall assessment

I'd rate this **8/10**. The concept is solid, but the garment sizing and alignment need refinement before it feels like a polished lookbook. Once you implement automatic scaling and body-zone anchoring, the experience will look much more professional and consistent.
