The client is essentially giving you a checklist of requirements and compliance items:

### 1. "All prices are inclusive of Tax so that needs to be mentioned alongside the item. This is a legal requirement"

**What they mean:**

* Every product page, product card, cart, and pricing display should clearly mention:

  * "Price Inclusive of GST"
  * "All Prices Include GST"
* Customers should know that the displayed price already includes tax.
* This is for legal and transparency reasons.

---

### 2. "I had request for color names to be added alongside each product so that customers can distinguish between a navy and a black since the images are very similar."

**What they mean:**

* Product images alone are not enough.
* Similar colors (Navy, Black, Charcoal, Dark Grey, etc.) are difficult to distinguish, especially on mobile devices.
* Display the color name directly below or beside each product.

**Example:**

* Men's Golf Polo – Navy
* Men's Golf Polo – Black
* Men's Golf Polo – Charcoal

---

### 3. "When I click on customise size it should go to the sizing page you have so elaborately created."

**What they mean:**

* The "Customize Size" button/link is not taking users to the size guide or sizing page.
* When users click it, they should be redirected to the dedicated sizing page that was previously created.

---

### 4. "At the back end the site should make an invoice for the customers purchase. There is a legal format for this invoice. Please ensure that is in place."

**What they mean:**

* Every successful order should generate a proper tax invoice.
* The invoice should follow the legally required format.
* It should include details such as:

  * Invoice Number
  * Order Number
  * Customer Details
  * Product Details
  * GST Breakdown
  * HSN Code
  * Shipping Charges
  * Total Amount
  * Seller Details
  * GST Number (if applicable)

---

### 5. "Please ensure that no customised item should be shipped via COD only prepaid."

**What they mean:**

* Any customized/personalized product should not allow Cash on Delivery (COD).
* Customers must pay online before placing the order.
* Standard/non-customized products may still allow COD if the business wants it.

**Expected Logic:**

```text
Customized Product
→ Prepaid Only

Regular Product
→ Prepaid + COD (if enabled)
```

### Summary

The client is asking for:

1. GST-inclusive price labels.
2. Visible color names for products.
3. Customize Size → redirect to sizing page.
4. Legally compliant invoice generation.
5. Customized products → prepaid only, no COD.
