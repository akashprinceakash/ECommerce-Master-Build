# 🧾 1. What “GST & Tax details” mean

Your client likely provided:

### 🏢 GSTIN (GST number)

Example:

* 29ABCDE1234F1Z5

This is the company’s tax identity.

---

### 💰 Tax structure (usually one of these)

They may define:

* **GST included in price (most common)**
* OR GST added at checkout

Typical GST rates:

* 5% (clothing essentials, some goods)
* 12%
* 18%
* 28%

---

# 🧠 2. You must decide how your system works

There are 2 models:

---

## ✅ Model A (Recommended): GST included in product price

Example:

* Product price = ₹1000 (GST already included)

You display:

* Price: ₹1000
* GST breakup (optional):

  * CGST 5%
  * SGST 5%

👉 Most Indian ecommerce uses this.

---

## ⚠️ Model B: GST added at checkout

Example:

* Product price = ₹1000
* GST 18% → ₹180
* Final = ₹1180

👉 This is less user-friendly.

---

# 🧾 3. What you must store in database

For each product/order:

### Product level:

* basePrice
* gstRate (e.g. 5, 12, 18)
* finalPrice

---

### Order level:

* subtotal
* gstTotal
* totalAmount
* invoiceNumber
* gstin (company)

---

# 📦 4. Invoice system (VERY IMPORTANT)

After payment success, you should generate invoice:

### Invoice must include:

* Company name + GSTIN
* Customer details
* Order ID
* Item list
* Tax breakup:

  * CGST
  * SGST (or IGST if interstate)
* Final total

👉 This is required for legal compliance.

---

# 📊 5. GST calculation logic (simple)

### If intra-state (same state):

* CGST + SGST (split GST)

### If inter-state:

* IGST only

# 🚚 6. How this connects to Razorpay

With Razorpay:

You should:

* Send **final total including GST**
* Store GST breakdown separately (for invoice)

Razorpay only cares about:
👉 final amount

---

# 📦 7. How this connects to Shiprocket

With Shiprocket:

Shipping system does NOT handle GST calculation.

But you must pass:

* invoice value
* declared value (important for courier insurance)

---

# 🧾 8. Admin panel update you should add

Admin should be able to:

* Set GST % per category
* Upload GSTIN
* View tax reports:

  * Monthly GST collected
  * Order-wise tax

---

# ⚠️ 9. Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Not storing GST separately
❌ Hardcoding tax in frontend
❌ Not generating invoice
❌ Mixing shipping + tax calculation
❌ Showing wrong price breakdown
